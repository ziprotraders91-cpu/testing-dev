FAQ

Aam sawal jo har Saree & Textile Karkhana aur Dukan maalik puchhta hai.

1. Naye Unified JobWork Trail se kya fayda hota hai?+
Ab aap unlimited naye work groups (jaise Swarovski, Jhalar, Stone) bana sakte hain, Outstation groups par Transport + JW No. + A5 Challan + A6 Packing Slip nikaal sakte hain, aur Owner Report me har saree ka stage trail aur 100% stock match ek saath dekh sakte hain.
2. Dukan ERP Management me Finished Stock aur Challan kaise jude hain?+
Karkhane me saree Ready/Finished hote hi woh automatic Dukan ERP ke Order Form, TSC 2-Up Barcode aur Challan me dikhne lagti hai. Challan save karte hi Finished Stock automatic minus hota hai, aur Edit/Delete karne par wapas restore bhi ho jaata hai!
3. Internet band ho gaya toh kya counter ruk jayega?+
Bilkul nahi! Isme Offline IndexedDB + Row-Delta Sync Queue laga hai. Bina net ke bhi entry aur print chalte rahenge, aur net aate hi automatic cloud par sync ho jayega.
4. Kya munshi ko Tally seekhna padega?+
Nahi! Poore software — chahe Karkhana Day-Book ho ya Dukan Day-Book — sabme Right-Side Live Picker aur 100% Zero-Mouse keyboard navigation hai. Sirf ↑↓ aur Enter se poora kaam hota hai.
