Complete Karkhana Workflow

Loom Se Lekar Dukan Tak Har Kadam Digital

Bina kisi password ke direct kisi bhi feature par click karein aur uska Ultra-HD interactive layout dekhein.

⚡ ZERO-MOUSE

⚡ JobWork Express (फास्ट काउन्टर)

F1-F6 keys se bina mouse chhue Cutting, Embroidery aur Calender ka hisab aur challan print.

  • ✔️ [F1] Cutting | [F2] Embroidery | [F3] Calender | [F4] Dukan
  • ✔️ [F5] Gayi/Aayi | [F6] Stock Source Switch
  • ✔️ Maker ➔ Naap (3.60) ➔ Qty ➔ Enter fast loop
  • ✔️ [Ctrl+Enter] Save & Print A6 Challan
⚡ SUPERFAST

⚡ Kariger Express (बैठक काउन्टर)

Baithak ke din 1-screen par Zero-Mouse hisab. Saree jama, Bana/Zari issue aur Rokad ek sath.

  • ✔️ 100% Zero-Mouse (Enter dabate jao agla box)
  • ✔️ 72 Pcs Tani Target & Loom par bachi saree meter
  • ✔️ 🎨 8 Colors matching list display
  • ✔️ Enter dabate hi turant A6 Thermal slip
⚡ INWARD COUNTER

⚡ Stock Express (कच्चा माल आवक)

Mandi ya tempo se aayi 10-15 bori Bana, Zari aur Sancha ek hi counter par 1 minute me stock me jama.

  • ✔️ 3-in-1 Engine: Bana, Zari aur Sancha
  • ✔️ [F1/F2/F3] Shortcuts se category switch
  • ✔️ Bacha wazan live screen par dikhna (502 Kg Bacha)
  • ✔️ Purana Stock ya +Naya Item mode
STEP 01

🧵 Kariger Master & Tani Khata

Har kariger ki Tani 1, 2, 3 sheets ka exact hisab. Mazdoori (Bani rate), 8 Colors matching aur Sancha record.

  • ✔️ 72 Saree Tani & Chukka Saree Setting
  • ✔️ Net Mazdoori = Net Saree × Bani Rate
  • ✔️ 15/30 din se absent kariger ka alert
STEP 02

🧶 Tana, Bana & Zari Issue

Kariger ko diye gaye daage ka pakka hisab. Less TP aur Topi ka wazan minus karke exact net issue nikalein.

  • ✔️ Net Bana = Cone - Less TP
  • ✔️ Net Zari = Qty - Topi (Wastage cut)
  • ✔️ Kariger return maal foran godam me jama
STEP 03

⚙️ Sancha (Tani & Chukka) System

Tani beam aur Chukka ka karkhana-wise alag-alag Kg hisab. Kis kariger ke paas kaun sa sancha hai, live tracking.

  • ✔️ Tani aur Chukka ke 2 alag-alag tabs
  • ✔️ Person-wise wazan ka hisab
  • ✔️ Godam se sancha issue hone par auto-minus
STEP 04

🥻 Saree Stock (5-Stage Pipeline)

Fresh ➔ Cutting ➔ Embroidery ➔ Calender ➔ Dukan. Har stage ka alag stock aur alag passbook khata.

  • ✔️ Fresh Godown, Cutting, Embroidery & Finished Tabs
  • ✔️ Har saree par stage-wise Dedicated Passbook
  • ✔️ 1 Godam se doosre godam transfer challan
STEP 05

✂️ Booti Cutting (Job Work)

Fresh stock se saree cutting ke liye bhejna. Single-line batch entry me sabhi kariger ka maal ek sath chadhayein.

  • ✔️ Kisko kitni saree di aur kab wapas aayi
  • ✔️ Damage saree aur cutting fine / kaat darj karein
  • ✔️ Cutting wale kariger ka monthly khata
STEP 06

🎛️ Calender Machine Finish (Ironing)

Badi-badi steam roller press machine me saree bhejna taaki unki uncreased fall aur silky chamak tayyar ho sake.

  • ✔️ Cutting, Embroidery ya Fresh se Calender bhejna
  • ✔️ Calender Finished hote hi seedhe Finished Stock
  • ✔️ Calender bills & payment voucher
Frequently Asked Questions

Aam Sawal Jo Har Karkhana Maalik Puchhta Hai

1. Agar karkhane me internet / Wi-Fi band ho gaya to kya kaam ruk jayega? +
Bilkul nahi! Simplicity ERP me Offline PWA Engine laga hua hai. Internet na hone par bhi aapka counter, entry aur print bina kisi rukawat ke chalte rahenge. Jaise hi internet aayega, saara data Supabase Cloud Server par automatically sync ho jayega.
2. Agar hamara mobile ya laptop toot gaya ya kho gaya to kya hisab chala jayega? +
Nahi! Aapka poora hisab 24×7 encrypted Cloud Database par surakshit rehta hai. Naye mobile ya computer me apna link aur PIN dalte hi 2 second me aapka poora karkhana waisa ka waisa wapas khul jayega.
3. Hamari purani diary ka bacha hua hisab isme kaise shuru hoga? +
Iske liye naye sire se poora panna nahi likhna padta. Pehle din har kariger ki pichli bachi mazdoori aur godam ka bacha Bana/Zari/Saree wazan 'Opening Stock' me daal diya jata hai, aur usi pal se naya hisab chalne lagta hai.
4. Kya munshi ko computer ya Tally seekhne ke liye alag se training leni padegi? +
Nahi! Yeh software khaas tor par karkhane ke munshiyon ke liye Hindi/Hinglish me banaya gaya hai. Sirf 'Enter' dabate jao aur dabba aage badhta jayega — 15 minute ke andar koi bhi aam munshi ise seekh kar fast entry shuru kar deta hai.
